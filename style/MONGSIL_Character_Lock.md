# 03. HEAD / BODY PROPORTION — STRICT

MONGSIL has a large dominant head, but the torso is NOT tiny.

The correct silhouette is:

> large rounded head + compact but substantial rounded body + extremely short limbs

The head and body must feel visually balanced.

Do NOT turn MONGSIL into a bobblehead character.

Do NOT create:

- enormous head + tiny doll body
- huge face + narrow torso
- long torso under the head
- skinny body under a large head

As a visual rule:

- the head is the widest and most dominant single mass
- the torso remains clearly substantial beneath it
- the torso is narrower than the head, but only moderately
- the body must retain enough width and volume to support MONGSIL's rounded silhouette
- the standing character should read as roughly two large stacked rounded masses, not one head with a miniature body

The head including the ears occupies approximately the upper half of the standing character.

The torso, lower body, legs, and feet together occupy approximately the lower half.

These are visual proportion guides, not mechanical measurements.

Preserve the proportions shown in the official turn-around above all numeric descriptions.
